# Resource Attribution and Reuse

- **MCFIX:** Eye-tracking data, stimulus text, and original annotations derive from [Li et al. (2023)](https://aclanthology.org/2023.vardial-1.12/) and the [MCFIX repository](https://github.com/CN-Eyetk/MCFIX). Included tables contain aggregate outcomes and derived features. No blanket MCFIX license was identified at preparation; this release does not assign new rights to those materials.
- **Cifu:** The lexicon derives from [Lai and Winterstein (2020)](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.375/) and [Cifu](https://github.com/gwinterstein/Cifu). The included version adds cleaned forms and PyCantonese POS/validity fields. Its upstream GPL-3.0 terms are preserved in `LICENSES/Cifu-GPL-3.0.txt`.
- **Models:** CKIP, JED351, Qwen2.5-7B, and CantoneseLLM-7B weights and tokenizers are obtained from their publishers and are not bundled. Their respective terms apply.
- **Software:** Third-party packages retain their original licenses. Text normalization follows the released MCFIX/Li-style workflow.

No separate blanket license has been selected for the authors' original code. Public access does not override third-party terms; contact the research authors for additional permissions.
